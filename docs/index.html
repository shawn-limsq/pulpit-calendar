LBPC Pulpit Schedule

Subscribe once. Any change to the preaching schedule appears in your calendar automatically.

Add to Apple Calendar Add to Google Calendar

Each service appears as an all-day event. Tap it to see the passage, sermon title and notes.

Google Calendar on a phone
  1. Google won't let you add calendars from its phone app. Open calendar.google.com in a browser instead (choose "Desktop site" on a phone).
  2. Next to Other calendars, tap +, then From URL.
  3. Paste the calendar link and tap Add calendar. It will then show in the Google Calendar app.
How often does it update?

Apple Calendar checks for updates based on your setting in Settings → Apps → Calendar → Accounts → Fetch New Data. Google Calendar checks on its own schedule, usually within a day.

How do I remove it?

Removing the calendar deletes all of its services from your calendar. Nothing else is affected.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the Calendar app and tap Calendars at the bottom.
  2. Tap the ⓘ next to the pulpit schedule.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Calendar.

Mac

  1. Open Calendar and show the calendar list (View → Show Calendar List).
  2. Right-click (or Control-click) the pulpit schedule and choose Unsubscribe.

Google Calendar

  1. Open calendar.google.com in a browser (choose "Desktop site" on a phone).
  2. Under Other calendars, point to the pulpit schedule and click the × (Unsubscribe), or open its ⋮ menu → Settings → Unsubscribe.
